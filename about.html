About us

This website aims to raise funds to help the Filipinos who live in Tondo, Manila who cannot afford food and are struggling with hunger. Through donations, people can contribute to providing meals and essential food supplies to those in need. Its goal is to fight Filipino hunger and ensure that everyone has access to nutritious and sufficient food.

Our Objectives

  • Eliminate hunger by 65% in Tondo, Manila
  • Serve nutritious food to hungry people
  • Unite organizations to help beat hunger

Our Mission

To fight hunger in Tondo, Manila by delivering essential food supplies and nutritious meals to low-income families and individuals in need, mobilizing community support and donations to build a future where no local resident suffers from food insecurity.

Our Vision

To establish a hunger-free Tondo where every resident, regardless of financial circumstance, has consistent access to sufficient, healthy, and nourishing food to thrive.
